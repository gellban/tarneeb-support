Help & support for the Tarneeb Master iOS game

Contact Us

If you have a question, found a bug, or need assistance with Tarneeb Master, please reach out to our support team. We aim to respond within 1–2 business days.

📧 support@tarneebmaster.com

How to Get Help

To help us resolve your issue as quickly as possible, please include the following details in your email:

  • Device model (e.g., iPhone 14, iPad Air 5)
  • iOS version (e.g., iOS 17.2)
  • App version (found in Settings → Apps → Tarneeb Master)
  • Description of the issue – what happened and how to reproduce it

Frequently Asked Questions

The app crashes or freezes

Try closing and reopening the app. If the problem persists, restart your device and ensure Tarneeb Master is updated to the latest version from the App Store. If you still experience crashes, please contact us with the details above.

I cannot connect to a game

Please check your internet connection and make sure you have a stable Wi-Fi or cellular signal. If connectivity issues continue, try toggling Airplane Mode off and on, then relaunch the app. You can also contact us if the problem continues.
