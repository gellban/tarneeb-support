How to Get Help

To help us resolve your issue quickly, please include the following information in your message:

  • Device model (e.g., iPhone 15, iPad Air)
  • iOS version (e.g., iOS 17.2)
  • App version (found in the App Store under your purchased apps)
  • Description of the issue — what happened, when it happens, and any steps to reproduce it

Frequently Asked Questions

The app crashes or freezes

Try closing the app completely and reopening it. If the problem continues, restart your device and ensure your iOS and Tarneeb Master are both up to date. If the issue persists, please contact us with your device model and iOS version.

I cannot connect to a game

Check that your device has an active internet connection. Try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data, or toggle Airplane Mode on and off. If you still cannot connect, please email us and we will look into it.
